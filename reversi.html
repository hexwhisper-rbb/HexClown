REVERSI

Capture your opponent's discs by trapping them between your own. Finish with the most discs to win!

⚫ BLACK PLAYER
2
⚪ WHITE PLAYER
2
⚫ Black Player's turn!
Black White Legal move

HOW TO PLAY

1. Black plays first.
2. Place a disc where it traps one or more opponent discs between your new disc and another of your discs.
3. All trapped opponent discs flip to your colour.
4. If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped.
5. When neither player can move, the player with the most discs wins.
