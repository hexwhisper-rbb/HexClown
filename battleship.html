HOW TO PLAY

1. Each player places five ships on their 10×10 ocean.
2. Ships can be placed horizontally or vertically.
3. Once both players are ready, take turns attacking the opponent's grid.
4. A hit means you found part of a ship. A miss means empty water.
5. Sink all five enemy ships to win!
