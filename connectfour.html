Connect four discs in a row before your opponent does. Think carefully and win!

HOW TO PLAY

1. Red plays first, followed by Yellow.
2. Tap one of the seven arrows to drop a disc.
3. Connect four discs horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.
4. If the board fills up without a winner, the game is a draw.
5. Use Undo to take back your last move.
