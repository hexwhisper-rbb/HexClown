RED PLAYER'S TURN

HOW TO PLAY

1. Red moves first.
2. Select one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square to move.
3. Regular pieces move diagonally forward by one square.
4. Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it.
5. Captures are compulsory. If a capture is available, you must make one.
6. Multiple captures are possible in one turn.
7. Reach the opposite end to become a KING. Kings move diagonally in both directions.
8. Capture all your opponent's pieces or leave them with no legal moves to win.
