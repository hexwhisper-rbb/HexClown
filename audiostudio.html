Record. Transform. Compose. Edit. Export.

8 AUDIO SYSTEMS AVAILABLE

01 / Voice Recorder & Live Waveform

Record your voice and watch the waveform respond in real time.

Recorder ready.

Microphone access is requested only when you start recording. Your recording stays in this browser until you export or save it in a project.

02 / Real-Time Voice Effects

Choose an effect before recording. The processed voice is sent to the recording output.

Effects are applied to the microphone signal. Helium and Monster use pitch shifting, which is approximated with playback-rate processing rather than studio-grade pitch correction.

03 / Text-to-Chiptune Generator

Turn your words into an original 8-bit melody.

Waiting for text.

04 / 8-Bit Beat & Step Sequencer

Build a beat by switching steps on and off. Each row is a different sound.

05 / Audio Trimmer & Silence Cropper

Load an audio file or use your recording. Trim it or automatically remove quiet sections at the beginning and end.

No audio loaded.

Silence cropping checks the beginning and end of the audio using a volume threshold. It does not remove quiet gaps in the middle.

06 / One-Click Audio Export

Export your recording, edited audio or generated beat as a WAV or MP3 file.

WAV export is built in. MP3 export needs the encoder library to load.

07 / Audio Project Save & Load

Save your current settings, beat pattern and available audio in this browser.

Projects are stored locally in this browser using IndexedDB. Clearing browser site data may erase them. Export important audio files as backups.

08 / BPM & Metronome Control

Set the tempo and use a metronome to keep time while creating music.
