TIC-TAC-TOE

Challenge a friend! Get three symbols in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

❌ PLAYER X
0
⭕ PLAYER O
0
❌ Player X's turn!

HOW TO PLAY

1. Player X starts the game.
2. Players take turns selecting empty squares.
3. Connect three matching symbols in a straight line to win.
4. If all nine squares are filled without a winner, it's a draw.
5. Use Undo to take back the last move.
