PIANO TILES

Tap the black tiles before they reach the bottom. Missing a tile ends the game!

Ready to play?

HOW TO PLAY

• Tap the black tiles as they appear.
• Don't let a black tile reach the bottom.
• Never tap the white spaces.
• Your score increases with every successful tap.
• The game gets faster as your score increases.
